General Questions

How do I know EliteBytes Tech is the right IT partner for my business?

We start with a free, no-obligation consultation no sales pitch, just honest advice. We assess your current IT setup, identify risks and gaps, and tell you exactly what you need and what you don't). If we're not the right fit, we'll tell you that too. Our goal is long-term partnership, not a one-time transaction.

Do you work with businesses that have no IT team at all?

Yes — and this is actually where we add the most value. Many of our clients come to us with zero internal IT support. We become your entire IT department: handling everything from day-to-day issues to long-term technology strategy,
at a fraction of the cost of hiring in-house.

What happens if something goes wrong after you deliver a project?

Every project includes post-delivery support. We don't disappear after launch. For managed IT clients, issues are covered under your plan. For project based work, we offer a support window and clear escalation paths. You'll always have a direct contact not a helpdesk ticket.

How quickly can you actually start working with us?

After your free consultation, most clients are onboarded within 3–5 business days. For urgent situations active cyberattacks, system failures, or critical deadlines we can mobilize within 24 hours. Speed is part of what you're paying for.

Do you sign NDAs and confidentiality agreements?

Yes, always. Before any discussion of your systems, data, or business processes, we sign a mutual NDA. Your business information never leaves our team without your explicit permission.

Can I see examples of your previous work?

Yes. We have case studies across eCommerce, financial services, logistics, and manufacturing available on request. Due to NDAs, some client names are anonymized but we can walk you through real results, timelines, and challenges solved during your consultation.

Popular Questions

What is the real ROI of outsourcing IT to EliteBytes Tech?

Our clients typically see: 40–60% reduction in IT-related downtime, 30% lower technology costs versus in-house teams, and faster resolution times for technical issues. Beyond numbers you get your team's focus back on growing the business, not fighting IT fires.

Why should I choose you over a local IT company in my country?

Three reasons: quality, speed, and price. Based in Europe with global delivery capabilities, we offer the same technical expertise as agencies in the US or UK at 40–60% lower cost. We work across time zones, communicate in English, and treat every client as a long-term partner regardless of geography.

What if I only need help with one specific thing not a full package?

That's completely fine. You don't have to commit to a full managed IT plan. We work on individual projects too a single Shopify store, one ad campaign, a cybersecurity audit, or a custom software feature.
Start small, scale when you're ready.

How do you handle communication and project updates?

You get a dedicated point of contact not a rotating helpdesk. We use Slack, email, or WhatsApp depending on your preference. For ongoing projects, you receive weekly status updates and have access to a shared project dashboard at all times.

What if I want to cancel or switch services?

No penalties, no drama. We operate on monthly rolling agreements for most services. If you want to pause, scale down, or cancel you give us 30 days notice and we handle the offboarding professionally, including full documentation and data handover.

What makes EliteBytes Tech different from other IT agencies?

Most agencies sell packages. We sell outcomes. We take time to understand your business before recommending any technology.
We measure success by your results reduced downtime, more sales, stronger security not by how many tools we deploy and we're small enough to care, experienced enough to deliver.
